# Disclaimer

The DPDP Self-Test Kit is a self-assessment aid published by volunteers.

- **It is not legal advice.** The controls and interpretation notes are community
  readings of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital
  Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. The Data Protection Board of India, the
  Appellate Tribunal and the courts may read the law differently.
- **It is not a certification, audit or endorsement.** No result from this kit
  means that an organisation, product or Consent Manager is compliant, approved,
  registered or recognised by the Data Protection Board or any other authority.
  Do not describe a result as a certification.
- **Automated checks have blind spots.** A PASS means only that a specific check
  found no gap under the conditions it ran in, such as one page load from one
  location, the tracker list at that time, and patterns the detector recognises.
- **Attestations are only as good as the person making them.** The kit records
  who attested what; it does not verify it.
- **The catalogue may lag the law.** Check the catalogue version in your report
  against current notifications, amendments and Board guidance.
